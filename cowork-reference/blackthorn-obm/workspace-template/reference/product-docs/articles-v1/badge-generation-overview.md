Blackthorn Badge Generation is a Salesforce-native tool that allows event teams to generate and print {{variable.Object_Attendee}} badges directly from {{variable.Product_Salesforce}}. It eliminates manual mail merges and third-party print tools by combining Salesforce Flows with Nintex DocGen templates. 

The simple, point-and-click process uses prebuilt templates and flows. Since the templates are Word-based, Event Organizers can easily modify them to update branding, {{variable.Object_Attendee}} fields, logos, and more. 

Users can then generate single badges or badges in bulk as PDF files, using {{variable.Object_Event}} and {{variable.Object_Attendee}} data that is already in {{variable.Product_Salesforce}}. Once the PDF is created, it’s as simple as opening it and using your existing printer and print process. 

## Benefits
* **Customize templates:** Word-based designs that support conditional logic, logos, headshots, and color cues.
* **Pre-event or bulk generation:** Create and regenerate badges for individuals or entire Attendee Lists with one click.
* **Maintain Branding:** Use centralized {{variable.Object_EventSettings}} to ensure consistent fonts, logos, and colors.
* **Salesforce-native:** Generate badges straight from your {{variable.Object_Event}} and {{variable.Object_Attendee}} records.

## Use Cases
* **Donor and VIP events:** Print 600+ badges for galas or university tailgates.
* **Conference and training management:** Generate badges for {{variable.Object_Speaker}}s, {{variable.Object_Staff}}, and {{variable.Object_Sponsor}}s.
* **Higher Education admissions events:** Customize badges as part of welcome packets for campus visit days and welcome weekends.
* **On-site reprints:** Instantly regenerate badges for lost or updated {{variable.Object_Attendee}}s.
* **Pre-event printing:** Batch-generate PDFs for smaller {{variable.Object_Event}}s or mail-outs.
