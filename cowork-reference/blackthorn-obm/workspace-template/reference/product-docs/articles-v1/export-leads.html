Prerequisite: Before you can export leads, please ensure you are part of an active team and have a Smart Capture event with captured leads in the app.

Note: Leads that say “needs credits” will not be exported until additional credits have been purchased.

  1. Select a Smart Capture event. 

  2. In the search bar, enter at least two characters to search by name or for a company. 

  3. Click the Export icon at the top of the screen. 

  4. Select Salesforce, CSV, or Sheets. Use the steps below to complete each process.

Export Leads to Salesforce

Prerequisite: Confirm you have the following before trying to export leads to {{variable.Product_Salesforce}}:

  • {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} Account (Production or Sandbox)

  • Appropriate permissions to create {{variable.Object_Lead}}s/{{variable.Object_Contact}}s and manage {{variable.Object_Campaign}}s

  • An active team and a Smart Capture event with captured leads in the app

Before you export leads to {{variable.Product_Salesforce}}, you have the option to either map Custom Question fields from a Smart Capture event to {{variable.Object_Lead}}s’ records and/or retrieve mappings from a different Smart Capture event to {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} for the new event.

Log in to Salesforce

  1. Log in to your {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} org.

  2. Enter your {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} credentials.

  3. Tap Allow to grant the Blackthorn Smart Capture app access to your org. Once the app is connected, it will present you with the option to select a {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} {{variable.Object_Campaign}} or map {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} fields.

  4. Enter the {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} {{variable.Object_Campaign}}’s {{variable.Field_Campaign_CampaignName}} (a partial name is okay) and select it.

  5. Now you can either tap the Export [X] Leads to Salesforce button to export leads or click Map Salesforce Fields to map fields to {{variable.Product_Salesforce}}.

Map Salesforce Fields

Map Custom Question Fields to Leads’ Records

You can now map leads’ fields from the Smart Capture app to the {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} {{variable.Object_Lead}} records’ fields. By default, fields in the Smart Capture app that lack a standard {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} field to map to are mapped to the {{variable.Field_Lead_Description}} field.

  1. Click Map Salesforce Fields.

  2. Scroll to the bottom to see Custom Question fields added in the Smart Capture app.

  3. Click Description (Description) next to the question/field you want to map.

  4. Select a field from the Salesforce {{variable.Object_Lead}} record.

  5. Click Save Mappings.

Retrieve Mappings from Another Event

In addition to mapping Custom Question fields from a Smart Capture event to {{variable.Product_Salesforce}}, you can also retrieve mappings from a different Smart Capture event to Salesforce for the new event.

  1. Click Map Salesforce Fields.

  2. Click the Share icon in the top-right corner of the screen.

  3. Select the Smart Capture event that contains the mapping you want to map for the new event.

  4. Click Apply.

Export Leads

Enter the {{variable.Product_Salesforce}} {{variable.Object_Campaign}}’s {{variable.Field_Campaign_CampaignName}} (a partial name is okay) and select it.

Tap the Export [X] Leads to Salesforce button.

Wait for the export to complete. This may take a few minutes.

Click OK once the export is complete.

When you go to the {{variable.Object_Campaign}}, you will see the {{variable.Object_Lead}}s in the {{variable.Object_CampaignMember}}s section.

Note: If duplicate {{variable.Object_Lead}}s are found, they will be attached to the {{variable.Object_Campaign}}.

Export Leads via CSV

  1. Tap the Export [X] Leads to Csv button. 

  2. Select where you would like to store the file.

Export Leads via Google Sheets

  1. Tap the Export [X] Leads to Sheets button. 

  2. Sign in to your Google account and continue to allow permissions. If you see “Google hasn’t verified this app,” go to Advanced, then “Go to Blackthorn | Smart Capture (unsafe),” and click Continue.

  3. The Google Sheet with your leads is exported to your Google account.

  4. Click OK once it’s finished, or click View to see the file now.
