Smart Capture Overview
The Blackthorn Smart Capture app is a universal lead scanning app that provides event professionals,
marketers, sales reps, and those responsible for fundraising/donor engagement with complete control
over their lead capture process.

Without relying on third-party tools or QR codes, users scan badges or business cards, while the AI-
powered enrichment tool creates robust lead entries that users can export to Salesforce in real-time.
The results include cleaner data, faster follow-ups, and more reliable ROI from every event.


Bene ts
Universal Scanning: Scan any attendee badge or business card; no QR codes or custom
hardware required. Manually push leads to Salesforce for immediate follow-up. (or use
without Salesforce)
AI-Powered Enrichment: Combine forces with AI. You scan a business card to gather the
name, company, and contact details. Our enrichment engine lls in missing email and details
so you never lose a valuable connection.
Full Data Ownership: You control what’s captured, enriched, and synced. Leads are
automatically backed up and accessible across all devices. With multiple export options, you
choose where to export your leads: to Salesforce, a CSV le, or Google Sheets.
Team Collaboration: Teams can utilize iOS and Android devices, enabling seamless
collaboration while sharing leads, events, and credits in real-time. Organize leads by event
and track which events generate the best connections.
Anywhere, Anytime: Capture leads at the booth, on the show oor, or at offsite events.
Never miss a lead due to poor connectivity. Smart Capture works of ine and syncs
automatically when you're back online.
Consistency Across Events: Standardize lead capture and quali cations.
Cost- and Time-Effective: Avoid expensive, rented scanners or fragmented third-party tools
and spend less time building your lead pro les.
Easy to Use: Minimal training is required, as the team uses a single process for every event.
Unlimited Access: Empower your entire team to scan, with no device restrictions.
Custom Fields: Qualify leads your way by capturing exactly the data your team needs.


Use Cases


Instant Lead Capture
You have a ve-person team attending this year’s networking conference. In previous years, everyone
would often lose a business card or two. This year, your employees no longer need to keep track of
business cards from leads/potential donors. They can easily scan attendee badges or business cards
directly from their phones, collecting accurate data.

Real-Time Prospect Insights
During a two-day event, you will meet a lot of prospects, some of whom already have
Account/Contact records in Salesforce. You run into someone whose name you recognize, but you
haven’t met. You scan their business card in the morning, review your Leads/potential donors in
Salesforce at lunch, and have a more in-depth and personal conversation by mid-afternoon.

Automated Lead Routing
Your organization is hosting a large gala event next month. While there will be many employees at the
event, only one person will be responsible for working with the new leads/potential donors after the
event. To simplify the process of gathering and collating information, scanned leads will automatically
be pushed to Salesforce campaigns, triggering work ows and assigning ownership of the records
without requiring manual effort.

Use At All Events
This year, your team will participate in 14 different events. Instead of rushing to become familiar with
14 different scanning systems, your team has one system to master. The consistency between events
helps them to accurately capture leads, enrich the data, and move quickly to the next steps.

Smart Capture FAQs

General Questions

What is Blackthorn Smart Capture?
Blackthorn Smart Capture is a universal lead scanning mobile app that allows event teams to capture
lead information instantly using their phone. The app scans badges or business cards and enriches the
data using AI without requiring QR codes.


Who can use Smart Capture?
Smart Capture is designed for event professionals, marketers, sales representatives, and anyone who
needs to capture and manage leads at trade shows, conferences, networking events, and other
business gatherings. While it can be used anywhere, it was speci cally designed for use at events.


What problems does Smart Capture solve?
The Smart Capture app helps solve multiple problems, including

follow-up delays due to waiting on data
inconsistent data gathered processes across events
expensive/fragmented solutions
lack of control and visibility into ROI


How does Smart Capture integrate with Salesforce?
Smart Capture is designed to work seamlessly with Salesforce, allowing you to:

Export leads directly to your Salesforce org
Map custom elds between the app and Salesforce
Maintain data consistency across platforms
Leverage existing lead management work ows


What devices support Smart Capture?
Smart Capture is available as a mobile app that can be downloaded from app stores. You can locate
and download the Blackthorn Smart Capture app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.


What information can be captured?
The Smart Capture app can extract various contact details from scanned materials, including:

Name and title
Company information
Email addresses
Phone numbers
Custom elds as con gured


How accurate is the data?
The data is 80% - 90% accurate but there is always a margin of error, especially since event attendees
don't always have the correct information on their badges. We highly recommend always reviewing
captured information before exporting to ensure accuracy.


Security

Is my data secure?
Yes, the Smart Capture app maintains enterprise-grade security standards and integrates securely with
Salesforce. All data transmission between the mobile app and your Salesforce org is encrypted and
follows industry best practices for data protection.


Credits

Do I need credits to use Smart Capture?
Yes, the Smart Capture app operates on a credit system. You need suf cient credits to enrich and
export leads. Any leads captured beyond your credit balance will not be enriched or exported until
enough credits have been purchased.


Salesforce Integration

What Salesforce authentication is required?
The Smart Capture app requires you to complete an authentication process during setup. This connects
the app to your Salesforce org and ensures secure data transfer between the mobile app and your
CRM system.


How does the Salesforce integration work?
Smart Capture’s Salesforce integration is a manual batch export, not an auto-sync. After capturing
leads at an event, users can export them by selecting Salesforce from the app’s export menu, logging
in, and choosing a Campaign. The leads are then created as Salesforce Leads and linked as Campaign
Members.

To prevent duplicate records, the logic rst looks to see which Salesforce Leads/Contacts exist and
creates Campaign Members based on them, then it creates any Leads/Campaign Members for Leads
that don't exist.

If duplicates are found, existing leads are attached to the campaign instead of recreated. Note that
standard Salesforce duplication rules may prevent some data (like notes) from transferring.

Additional emails are added to the Desc riptio n eld. Currently, there’s no support for record types or
custom elds. Events can be exported multiple times to different orgs or Campaigns, and users are
automatically signed out after a period.

Manage and Export Leads

How is information added to the Lead's Description eld?
For example, when multiple Custom Question elds write to the Desc riptio n on a Lead or Contact
record, do they overwrite each other or is each value (answer) added?

All values are placed together in the Salesforce Lead record’s Desc riptio n eld, nothing is
overwritten. This allows an organization to capture all of the data without having to create multiple
Salesforce custom elds in their org to map to. Alternatively, the gathered data can remain in the app
by setting the question to "Skip this eld" during the export process.


What happens to the Description eld when multiple
exports occur?
If the same lead and same event are exported multiple times, the previous value in the Lead's
Desc riptio n eld will NOT be updated, even if the values in the Custom Question elds are changed.

However, if you capture the same lead in a different event, the Lead’s Desc riptio n eld WILL be
updated with the new information upon export.


Can I manage Leads of ine?
Smart Capture allows you to scan, capture, and manage leads even when of ine. The leads will be
stored locally on your device and can be exported to Salesforce once you regain internet connectivity.


What happens if I don't have enough credits?
If you don't have suf cient credits, leads will still be captured but won't be enriched or exported to
your CRM until enough credits are purchased. The app will display a noti cation when you're running
low on credits.

including the capture, update, and deletion of leads, as well as the deletion of Smart Capture events.


Purchase Credits
The Purchase Credits screen displays your current balance and lets you purchase new credits via Stripe
(on U.S. iOS devices) or via an in-app purchase.

Note: You can still capture leads if the credit balance is 0. However, any leads in excess of the credit
balance will not be enriched or exported until enough credits have been purchased.


Smart Capture Logic Explanation

Salesforce Export Flow
Existing-record detection happens up front: populateLookupCache queries both Lead and
Contact objects by email (or rst+last+company) so every Smart Capture lead knows if
Salesforce already has a Lead, a converted Lead, or a Contact counterpart before any inserts
run (functions/exportService.js:538-768).
Each lead is turned into a state with a captured-at marker plus any elds that should live in
Description; the matching record metadata (type, Salesforce Id, prior Description) is stored on
that state (functions/exportService.js:2075-2119).
States that have no match are batched through a Bulk API insert job; the CSV rows honor
campaign-speci c eld mappings so standard and custom slots can target any writable Lead
eld—not just Description (functions/salesforceBulk.js:154-274,
functions/exportService.js:2138-2149).
Records that already exist (either directly from the cache or surfaced as duplicate errors from
the insert job) skip eld updates entirely; the service only re-attaches them to the chosen
campaign and earmarks them for a description append (functions/exportService.js:1474-2249).


Updates vs. Description Appends
There is no eld-level merge for existing Leads or Contacts; instead
appendDescriptionsForExistingRecords merges the Smart Capture block into the existing
Description (32 kB cap) and skips if the same lead marker is already present
(functions/exportService.js:2401-2360).
Custom elds only land in Salesforce outside Description when the admin maps custom.*
slots to real Lead columns before the export; otherwise they’re stringi ed and appended
(with labels) to the Description so historical data isn’t overwritten
(functions/salesforceBulk.js:234-294).
Because only brand-new records go through Bulk insert, there’s no risk of overwriting
Salesforce-owned elds, and there is no “append-only” limitation for those fresh records—
they carry every mapped eld in the CSV payload (functions/exportService.js:2524-2594).


Converted Lead Handling
Salesforce forbids updating a converted Lead record; the export code treats any match with


IsConverted or a plain Contact as a Contact from the start, so campaign attachments use
ContactId instead of LeadId (functions/exportService.js:1431-2144).
If the Bulk CampaignMember insert still hits CANNOT_UPDATE_CONVERTED_LEAD, it
fetches the related ConvertedContactId and retries the attachment as a Contact before
agging an error (functions/exportService.js:2669-2690).
Description updates follow the same rule: if a Lead description update fails with
CANNOT_UPDATE_CONVERTED_LEAD, the code refetches the converted Contact Id and
reruns the update against the Contact object, otherwise it records a failure
(functions/exportService.js:2441-2477).
